## Issue log

### A. Blocking, evidenced

**A1 — Project Intelligence "WHERE TO LOOK FIRST" mixes two taxonomies.**
Eight buckets summing to 3,054: Early window 180, Later window 287, Long-dated
1,196, In planning 960, Under construction 238, Register: consent not live 172,
Consented date not published 20, Unclassified 1. The heading claims projects
"placed in the window between consent and construction", but 1,391 of them are
not in that window at all — they are in planning, under construction, or have no
consent date. Window position and planning status are two different questions
presented as one list. "Long-dated" is the largest bucket and is never defined.
"Unclassified 1" is a bucket of one.

**A2 — Relationship Evidence answers nothing.**
Three rows, all `decision: ABSTAIN`, all `eligible_for_join: false`. It renders
an internal governance ledger — `CANDIDATE_ONLY_NOT_CONFIRMED_OWNERSHIP`,
`UNASSERTED_OWNER_OR_OPERATOR_SLOT`, 482,030 candidate rows — behind a button
labelled OPEN RELATIONSHIP EVIDENCE. A reader asking who owns a project learns
nothing. The caveats are honest; the panel is not information.

**A3 — Grid proximity grades projects.**
`STRONG / MODERATE / DISTANT / REMOTE`, with green-to-red colouring. That is a
verdict on a project's grid position, not a measurement, and it is exactly what
must not be implied: a developer may build their own substation, and distance to
a mapped asset says nothing about whether they will. Report the distance; never
grade it. Same for any "target acquired" phrasing.

## The scanner

A relevance scanner for the news collector, replacing keyword scraping.

**Reject first, cheaply.** Off-topic terms (care home, ring road, port),
geopolitics (this page is engineering and business), and any item whose only
match is a bare planning-portal listing.

**Then score for relevance, aggressively, on solar PV and grid.**

| Signal | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| REPD ref present and resolvable | decisive | It is about a known project. Bind it. |
| Named capacity ≥ 50 MW | high | Large projects are the subject. |
| Named capacity 1–50 MW | medium | |
| Developer/operator matching the REPD operator set | high | |
| Grid vocabulary: substation, connection offer, curtailment, DNO, TO, grid-forming, inverter, transformer | high | The engineering the estate is for. |
| Document depth: DCO, s36, planning decision letter, financial close, EPC award, PPA | high | Detail, not a portal stub. |
| Bare portal listing with no capacity, developer or decision | reject | The noise this is meant to remove. |

**Two outputs, never merged.** `BOUND` carries a project caption. `SECTOR`
carries none. Everything else is withheld with a reason recorded.

**Where it runs.** At collection, in the payload builder, so the classification
is data rather than a render-time regex. Tonight's filter is a render-time
stopgap and should be replaced by this.
